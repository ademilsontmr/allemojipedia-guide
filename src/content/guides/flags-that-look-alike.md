Most look-alike flags can be separated by checking one detail: the order of the stripes, the color nearest the pole, or a small emblem in the center. A few pairs, including Chad and Romania and Indonesia and Monaco, are close to identical at emoji size, and for those the only reliable method is to check the emoji's name before you send it.

This guide groups the commonly confused flag emojis by pattern and gives the single fastest tell for each. Flags are described as they appear on screen, with the pole (the hoist) on the left.

## Why flags are harder to tell apart as emojis

A real flag is a meter wide. A flag emoji is drawn in a space about the height of a line of text, which removes exactly the details that distinguish similar flags.

- **Shades disappear.** The difference between Chad's darker blue and Romania's medium blue, or between the Netherlands' blue and Luxembourg's lighter blue, is subtle in cloth and nearly invisible in a tiny icon. Each platform also picks its own shades.
- **Proportions are flattened.** Monaco's flag is officially closer to square than Indonesia's, and Switzerland's is a true square. Most emoji sets draw every flag at the same width, with Switzerland as an occasional exception.
- **Emblems shrink to smudges.** A coat of arms becomes a few colored pixels.
- **Styles differ by platform.** Some vendors draw flags as waving cloth, which bends stripes and hides corners; others draw flat rectangles. The same flag can be easier to identify on one phone than another, as described in [iPhone vs Android emojis](/guides/iphone-vs-android-emojis/).

Underneath the picture, each flag is a pair of letters that spell a region code. That is useful here, and the last section explains how. The mechanics are covered in [flag emojis explained](/guides/flag-emojis-explained/).

## The near-identical pairs

### Chad 🇹🇩 and Romania 🇷🇴

Both are vertical tricolors of blue, yellow and red. Chad's blue is officially a darker indigo; Romania's is a medium cobalt. Romania used a version with a communist emblem in the center until 1989, and when it returned to the plain tricolor the two flags became almost the same. Neither country has changed its design since.

**Tell:** there is no dependable visual tell at emoji size. Check the name.

Two neighbors share the layout with an added emblem: **Andorra 🇦🇩** and **Moldova 🇲🇩** both have a coat of arms on the yellow stripe. If the center is not plain, it is one of those two.

### Indonesia 🇮🇩, Monaco 🇲🇨 and Poland 🇵🇱

Indonesia and Monaco are both red over white. Poland is the reverse, white over red.

**Tell:** red on top means Indonesia or Monaco, and you need the name to know which. White on top means Poland. A memory aid for Poland: white sky over red earth is upside down from the other two.

**Singapore 🇸🇬** is also red over white, but it has a white crescent and five stars in the upper left. **Greenland 🇬🇱** is white over red with a circle split between the two colors.

### Haiti 🇭🇹 and Liechtenstein 🇱🇮

Both are blue over red. The story goes that the two countries discovered the clash at the 1936 Olympics, and Liechtenstein added a gold crown in the upper left corner the following year. Haiti's national flag has a coat of arms on a white panel in the center.

**Tell:** crown in the corner is Liechtenstein; emblem in the middle is Haiti.

## Mirrored and rotated tricolors

These are the mix-ups that happen most in practice, because the flags are popular and the colors match.

### Ireland 🇮🇪 and Ivory Coast 🇨🇮

Same three colors, opposite order. The [Ireland flag](/emojis/flag-ireland/) is green, white, orange. Ivory Coast (Côte d'Ivoire) is orange, white, green.

**Tell:** green next to the pole is Ireland. This is the single most common flag emoji mistake around St. Patrick's Day, often next to a [four leaf clover](/emojis/four-leaf-clover/), so check before you post.

### Italy 🇮🇹, Mexico 🇲🇽 and Hungary 🇭🇺

The [Italy flag](/emojis/flag-italy/) is a plain vertical green, white and red. The [Mexico flag](/emojis/flag-mexico/) uses the same colors with the national coat of arms, an eagle on a cactus, in the center. Hungary uses red, white and green in horizontal bands.

**Tell:** emblem in the middle is Mexico; plain vertical is Italy; horizontal is Hungary. **Bulgaria 🇧🇬** is horizontal white, green, red, and **Iran 🇮🇷** is horizontal green, white, red with a red emblem.

### India 🇮🇳 and Niger 🇳🇪

Both are horizontal orange, white and green. The [India flag](/emojis/flag-india/) has a navy blue wheel, the Ashoka Chakra, in the center. Niger has an orange disc.

**Tell:** blue wheel is India; orange dot is Niger.

### France 🇫🇷, the Netherlands 🇳🇱, Luxembourg 🇱🇺 and Russia 🇷🇺

All red, white and blue. The [France flag](/emojis/flag-france/) is vertical: blue, white, red. The other three are horizontal:

- **Netherlands:** red, white, blue from the top.
- **Luxembourg:** the same order with a lighter, sky blue.
- **Russia:** white, blue, red from the top.

**Tell:** vertical is France. Red on top is the Netherlands or Luxembourg (lighter blue). White on top is Russia.

### Belgium 🇧🇪 and Germany 🇩🇪

Both use black, red and gold. The [Germany flag](/emojis/flag-germany/) is horizontal: black, red, gold from the top. Belgium is vertical: black, yellow, red from the pole.

**Tell:** horizontal is Germany, vertical is Belgium.

## Same flag, different emblem

When two flags share a background, look at the center or the upper left.

**Slovakia 🇸🇰, Slovenia 🇸🇮 and Russia 🇷🇺.** All three are white, blue and red horizontal bands. Slovakia has a shield with a white double cross; Slovenia has a smaller shield with a white mountain in the upper left. Russia is plain.

**Croatia 🇭🇷 and Paraguay 🇵🇾.** Both add a central emblem to red, white and blue bands. Croatia's is a red and white checkerboard shield, usually visible even when small. Paraguay's is a round seal.

**Colombia 🇨🇴, Ecuador 🇪🇨 and Venezuela 🇻🇪.** All are yellow, blue and red horizontal bands, a shared inheritance from Gran Colombia. Colombia is plain with a double-height yellow band. Ecuador is the same with a coat of arms in the center. Venezuela has three equal bands and an arc of white stars on the blue.

**Honduras 🇭🇳, El Salvador 🇸🇻 and Nicaragua 🇳🇮.** All are blue, white, blue horizontal bands. Honduras has five blue stars in the center. El Salvador and Nicaragua each have a triangular national emblem in the center and are very hard to separate as emojis. **Argentina 🇦🇷** has lighter blue bands and a golden sun.

**Senegal 🇸🇳, Mali 🇲🇱 and Guinea 🇬🇳.** Mali is vertical green, yellow, red. Senegal is the same with a green star on the yellow. Guinea is the mirror image: red, yellow, green. **Cameroon 🇨🇲** is green, red, yellow with a yellow star in the middle.

**Egypt 🇪🇬, Yemen 🇾🇪 and Iraq 🇮🇶.** All are red, white and black horizontal bands. Yemen is plain. Egypt has a golden eagle in the center. Iraq has green Arabic script. Syria's flag emoji may appear as either of two different designs depending on how recently a platform has updated its artwork, so treat it with extra care.

**Jordan 🇯🇴, the Palestinian Territories 🇵🇸 and Sudan 🇸🇩.** Jordan and the [Palestinian flag](/emojis/flag-palestinian-territories/) both have black, white and green bands with a red triangle at the pole; Jordan adds a small white star inside the triangle. Sudan has red, white and black bands with a green triangle.

## Shared cantons, crosses and discs

### Australia 🇦🇺 and New Zealand 🇳🇿

Both are blue ensigns with the Union Jack in the upper left and the Southern Cross on the right. The [Australia flag](/emojis/flag-australia/) has six white stars, including a large seven-pointed star under the Union Jack. New Zealand has four red stars outlined in white.

**Tell:** white stars are Australia, red stars are New Zealand. Several smaller flags use the same layout on a lighter blue with a shield or stars, including Fiji and Tuvalu.

### United States 🇺🇸, Liberia 🇱🇷 and Malaysia 🇲🇾

Red and white stripes with a blue canton. The [United States flag](/emojis/flag-united-states/) has fifty stars. Liberia has a single large white star. Malaysia has a yellow crescent and star.

**Tell:** look at the canton. One star is Liberia; yellow is Malaysia.

### Puerto Rico 🇵🇷 and Cuba 🇨🇺

The two designs are color-swapped versions of each other. The [Puerto Rico flag](/emojis/flag-puerto-rico/) has red and white stripes with a blue triangle. Cuba has blue and white stripes with a red triangle. Both triangles hold a white star.

**Tell:** blue triangle is Puerto Rico; red triangle is Cuba.

### The Nordic crosses

Five countries share an off-center cross design.

| Flag | Background | Cross |
|---|---|---|
| Denmark 🇩🇰 | Red | White |
| Norway 🇳🇴 | Red | Blue with white outline |
| Iceland 🇮🇸 | Blue | Red with white outline |
| Sweden 🇸🇪 | Blue | Yellow |
| Finland 🇫🇮 | White | Blue |

Norway and Iceland are the confusing pair: they are color-swapped. A red background is Norway; a blue background is Iceland. Separately, **Switzerland 🇨🇭** (a centered white cross on red) is sometimes confused with Denmark, and with the red cross symbol, which is not a flag at all.

### Discs and crescents

- **Japan 🇯🇵, Bangladesh 🇧🇩 and Palau 🇵🇼.** The [Japan flag](/emojis/flag-japan/) is a red disc on white. Bangladesh is a red disc on green. Palau is a yellow disc on light blue.
- **Turkey 🇹🇷 and Tunisia 🇹🇳.** Both are red with a white crescent and star. On Turkey's flag the crescent and star are white directly on the red. On Tunisia's they are red, inside a white disc.
- **Qatar 🇶🇦 and Bahrain 🇧🇭.** Both have a white band at the pole with a zigzag edge. Qatar's main color is maroon; Bahrain's is bright red.

### Thailand 🇹🇭 and Costa Rica 🇨🇷

Five horizontal stripes in red, white and blue. Thailand has a wide blue stripe in the middle with red on the outside. Costa Rica has a wide red stripe in the middle with blue on the outside.

**Tell:** blue in the middle is Thailand, red in the middle is Costa Rica.

## Flag emojis that really are identical

A few flag emojis are different characters with the same picture. Some territories have their own region code but fly the flag of the governing country, so most platforms draw them identically:

- Bouvet Island and Svalbard and Jan Mayen appear as the Norway flag.
- The US Outlying Islands appear as the US flag.
- Heard and McDonald Islands appear as the Australia flag.
- Clipperton Island and Saint Martin usually appear as the France flag.

This matters in two situations. If you pick a flag by eye from the full grid, you may choose the territory version, which screen readers announce by its real name and which may not match in search. And if you are building anything that filters by country, the two are not interchangeable.

## How to check a flag before you send it

The picture is the least reliable way to identify a flag emoji. These methods are better.

1. **Search by name.** On iPhone, Android and most desktop pickers, type the country name in the emoji search field. You get the right flag without comparing stripes. Steps for each system are in [how to type emojis on any device](/guides/how-to-type-emojis-on-any-device/).
2. **Read the label.** Hovering over an emoji in desktop pickers, or pressing and holding in some mobile apps, shows its name. Every flag is named "flag:" followed by the region.
3. **Use the letters.** On Windows, country flags are displayed as their two-letter codes in most programs. This is normally a nuisance, but for look-alikes it is a precise answer: `RO` is Romania and `TD` is Chad; `ID` is Indonesia, `MC` is Monaco and `PL` is Poland; `IE` is Ireland and `CI` is Ivory Coast.
4. **Add the country name** in text when the flag matters, such as in an event announcement or a product listing. It also helps anyone whose device shows the flag differently and anyone using a screen reader.

Mistakes with flags tend to be noticed, because people care about their own. Sending the wrong tricolor to a colleague or customer is a small error, but it is an easy one to avoid.
