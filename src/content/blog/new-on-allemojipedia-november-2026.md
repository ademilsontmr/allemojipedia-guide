This week Allemojipedia gained a tool that explains any emoji you paste into it, a set of copy-and-paste pages for kaomoji and text symbols, 60 new emoji meaning pages, three new guides and the blog you are reading now. Here is what each one does.

## Paste an emoji, get its meaning

The new [emoji meaning finder](/what-does-this-emoji-mean/) answers the question people ask most: what does this emoji mean? Copy an emoji from a message, or the whole message, paste it in and press Find meaning. Every emoji in the text is picked out and explained once, in order: its official name, its category, the year it was added and, for the emojis that have a meaning page, a plain-English summary of how people actually use it, with a link to the full explanation.

It recognizes skin-tone versions (👍🏽 is treated as 👍), flags and every one of the 1,923 emojis in the current standard. It runs in your browser; nothing you paste is sent to us.

## Kaomoji and text symbols to copy

Not everything people paste into a bio or a chat is an emoji. Five new pages collect the characters people search for most, grouped the way you would look for them, with one tap to copy:

- [Kaomoji](/kaomoji/): Japanese-style text faces such as (^_^) and ¯\\\_(ツ)\_/¯, sorted by emotion.
- [Copy and paste symbols](/text-symbols/): checkmarks, arrows, math, currency, brackets, music, chess and card symbols.
- [Cute symbols](/cute-symbols/): tiny hearts, flowers, sparkles, frames and ready-made bio combos.
- [Heart symbols](/heart-symbols/): text hearts such as ♡ and ღ next to every heart emoji.
- [Star symbols](/star-symbols/): ★ ☆ ✦ ✧, sparkles and rating stars.

Each page explains where the symbols display reliably, how to type them without copy and paste, and how screen readers read them aloud.

## 60 new emoji meaning pages

Allemojipedia has always written fewer, deeper pages rather than one thin page for every emoji. This week 60 more emojis got that treatment, chosen because people search for them often. They include the [woman shrugging](/emojis/woman-shrugging/), the [anatomical heart](/emojis/anatomical-heart/), the [hamsa](/emojis/hamsa/), the [red envelope](/emojis/red-envelope/), the [green square](/emojis/green-square/) that fills Wordle grids, the crab, [sushi](/emojis/sushi/) and [bubbles](/emojis/bubbles/). There are now 532 emoji meaning pages, each covering how the emoji is used in texts and online, real example messages, combos, look-alikes and when not to send it. Every one is listed on [all emoji meanings](/emojis/).

Every meaning page now also leads with a one-tap copy button, so the page you land on to learn what an emoji means is also the quickest place to copy it.

Guides explain things that stay true. This blog is for things that change: new Unicode releases, when new emojis reach each platform, and news about the site. The first report covers [Emoji 18.0](/blog/emoji-18-0-new-emojis/), the nine new emojis approved this year. The blog also has an RSS feed if you want new posts in your reader.
