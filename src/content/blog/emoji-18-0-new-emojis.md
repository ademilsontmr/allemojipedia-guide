Emoji 18.0, part of the Unicode release published in September 2026, adds **nine new emojis**: a cracking face, two sideways thumbs, a monarch butterfly, a pickle, a lighthouse, a meteor, an eraser and a net with handle. Counting the skin-tone versions of the two thumbs, that is **19 new characters** in total, according to the Unicode Consortium's official list.

They are approved, but you probably cannot send them yet. Phone and computer makers add new emojis through system updates over the following months, so most people will start seeing them during 2027. Until a device is updated, a new emoji shows up as an empty box or a question mark.

## The full Emoji 18.0 list

| Emoji | Unicode name | Code point | Category | Unicode proposal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cracking face | cracking face | U+1FAEB | Smileys & Emotion | L2/26-048 |
| Leftwards thumb | leftwards thumb sign | U+1FAF9 | People & Body | L2/25-252 |
| Rightwards thumb | rightwards thumb sign | U+1FAFA | People & Body | L2/25-252 |
| Monarch butterfly | monarch butterfly | U+1FACC | Animals & Nature | L2/25-254 |
| Pickle | pickle | U+1FADD | Food & Drink | L2/25-253 |
| Lighthouse | lighthouse | U+1F6D9 | Travel & Places | L2/25-256 |
| Meteor | meteor | U+1FA8B | Travel & Places | L2/25-257 |
| Eraser | eraser | U+1FA8C | Objects | L2/25-255 |
| Net with handle | net with handle | U+1FA8D | Objects | L2/25-258 |

The two thumbs each come in the five standard skin tones, which accounts for the other ten characters. Every other new emoji is a single character with no variants.

We have not pasted the new characters into this article on purpose: on most devices today they would only display as boxes.

## The faces and hands

**Cracking face** is the only new smiley. Unicode's keyword list for it includes "breaking", "crumbling", "masking", "resilience" and "reveal", which points to the two ways people are likely to use it: a composure that is about to give way, and a mask coming off to show what is underneath. It joins a group of strained, half-hidden faces that already includes the [melting face](/emojis/melting-face/), the [dotted line face](/emojis/dotted-line-face/) and the [face with diagonal mouth](/emojis/face-with-diagonal-mouth/), all of which people use to say "I am fine" when they are not.

**Leftwards and rightwards thumb sign** are thumbs held sideways, neither up nor down. The keywords are "neutral", "point", "this" and "that". Today the closest options are the [thumbs up](/emojis/thumbs-up/) and the pointing hands such as [👉](/emojis/backhand-index-pointing-right/), but a thumb jerked to the side reads differently: a casual "over there", a "meh" that is softer than a thumbs down, or a way to point at the message above or below.

## Nature, food and the sky

**Monarch butterfly** is a specific species, orange and black, rather than a second generic [butterfly](/emojis/butterfly/). Its keywords lean on "metamorphosis", "migration", "hope" and "transformation", so expect it in posts about personal change as much as in nature photography.

**Pickle** is the release's food item. The keyword list includes "brine", "ferment", "gherkin" and "relish", and also "trouble", a nod to the idiom "in a pickle". Until it arrives, people will keep reaching for the cucumber 🥒, which is a different thing.

**Meteor** is a falling rock in flames, tagged with "fireball", "apocalypse" and "space". It sits beside the [shooting star](/emojis/shooting-star/) and the comet ☄️; the shooting star is usually for wishes and wonder, while a meteor carries more sense of impact, which is likely to make it a reaction to bad news or chaos.

**Lighthouse** is listed in the travel section, with keywords such as "beacon", "guidance", "hope" and "navigation". Coastal travel photos are the obvious use; "you are my lighthouse" style messages are the sentimental one.

## Two new objects

**Eraser** comes with "correction", "delete", "mistake" and "undo", a natural reply to a typo or a message you wish you had not sent. **Net with handle** is the kind used for butterflies, fish or bugs, tagged "catch", "collect" and "trap".

## When you can use them

Approval by Unicode is only the first step. Each platform then has to draw its own designs and ship them in a system update, and the timing differs from company to company and from year to year. In practice:

- **Phones and computers** get new emojis through an operating system update, not through a keyboard app or an app update.
- **Messaging apps that use their own emoji artwork** can show new designs on their own schedule, but the person you are messaging still needs a device that knows the character.
- **Older devices** that stop receiving updates never gain the new emojis. Our guide to [why emojis show as boxes](/guides/why-emojis-show-as-boxes/) explains what the other person sees.

If you want the background on how a proposal becomes an emoji, including what those L2 proposal numbers mean, read [how new emojis get approved](/guides/how-new-emojis-get-approved/). The emojis that reached phones this year came from the previous release; they are covered in [new emojis in 2026](/guides/new-emojis-2026/).

## What happens next

The Unicode Consortium accepts new proposals every year and publishes draft candidates for the following release, which then go through review before final approval. We will report the next set here on the [blog](/blog/) once Unicode publishes it, and add meaning pages for the Emoji 18.0 characters as they become usable on common devices.
